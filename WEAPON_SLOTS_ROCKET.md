# Weapon slots and the rocket launcher

Not tested in the Godot editor. Open the project once (imports the new PNGs, registers the new classes), then check
the Output panel.

## Weapon slots (keys 1 / 2 / 3)
- **1 = sword** (always available), **2 = AK-47**, **3 = rocket launcher**. New input actions `weapon_1..3`.
  (You wrote "additional weapon1" and "weapon3"; I read that as the two additional weapons in slots 2 and 3.)
- **One weapon of each type:** picking up a weapon you already carry only adds ammo (AK-47 +30 up to 300, rocket launcher
  +1 up to 5). A full weapon refuses the pickup (it stays on the ground; the shop shows "Full").
- The first pickup of a weapon puts it into its slot and selects it. Pressing the key of an empty slot shows
  "No weapon in slot N". When a weapon's ammo runs out it is thrown away and the slot is free again; if it was selected, the
  sword is selected.
- **Hotbar** at the bottom center: the three slots with icons, the key number and the ammo; the selected slot has a yellow
  frame and empty slots are dimmed. The "AK-47: n / 300" line in the bottom right is gone (the hotbar shows it).
- New icons: `assets/weapons/sword.png`, `rocket_launcher.png` (the AK-47 icon already existed).
- Code: `weapons/player_weapon.gd` is the base class (ammo, fire rate, automatic or one shot per click, animation names, throw
  away when empty); `Ak47Weapon` and `RocketLauncherWeapon` extend it. A new weapon = a subclass plus an item and a slot.

## Rocket launcher
- **Pickup:** drop chance **5 %** from every enemy (both drop tables, adjustable in ESC > Developer > Drops). One rocket per
  pickup, maximum 5. It is also in the shop (8 coins, my choice, since the shop sells "the weapons" and the health pack).
- **Fire:** one rocket per click (1.2 s between shots). The rocket flies at 230 px/s with an animated flame and a smoke trail
  and explodes when it hits an enemy, a wall or a house, or at the end of its range (370 px).
- **Damage 500, two radii** (exports on `weapons/rocket_launcher_weapon.gd`):
  - first radius **50 px**: 500 damage,
  - second radius **80 px**: **half damage (250)**, and because it also contains the first radius it is added there.
  - So: inside 50 px = 500 + 250 = **750**, between 50 and 80 px = **250**, outside 80 px = nothing.
  Enemy armor applies to the total, so with the rarity scaling a Mythical enemy survives a rocket in the inner zone and a
  Legendary one survives it in the outer ring. The explosion does not hurt the player.
- **Knockback** for survivors: 40 px.

## Sprites and animations (all placeholder art, generated by `tools/make_weapon_art.py`)
- **Player:** `assets/characters/player_rocket.png`: the hooded warrior with the launcher on the shoulder. Animations
  `idle_rocket` (4 frames) and `fire_rocket` (4 frames: loaded, flash with recoil and backblast smoke, empty tube).
  When aiming mostly up or down the turned back / front body is used with the launcher icon pointing at the mouse (same as
  the AK-47).
- **Rocket:** `assets/weapons/rocket_frames.png`: 3 flickering flame frames, plus a grey smoke trail.
- **Explosion:** `assets/weapons/explosion_sheet.png`: 9 frames (flash, fireball, cooling, smoke) at 18 fps, scaled to the first
  radius. Also two expanding shockwave rings (one per damage radius), sparks, rising smoke, falling debris and a pixel-pattern
  scorch mark on the ground (random like the water splash).
- **Launch:** backblast fire and smoke behind the player, flash at the muzzle.
