# Weapon classes, two death animations, the coin counter

Not tested in the Godot editor. Open the project once (registers the new classes, imports the new PNGs), then check the
Output panel. This replaces the death and coin parts of `DEATH_DROPS_HUD_CAMERA.md`.

## Weapon classes
- New `WeaponClass` (`combat/weapon_class.gd`) with two types:
  - **Light:** sword, AK-47
  - **Heavy:** rocket launcher
- Every hit carries the class of the weapon that dealt it (`HitInfo.weapon_class`). Weapons set it in one place:
  `PlayerWeapon.weapon_class` (AK-47 light, rocket launcher heavy), `attack.gd` for the sword. A new weapon just picks a class.
  Bullets and rockets carry it from the weapon into the hit; a rocket explosion is heavy.
- Everything else counts as light: the thrown bottle, teleport blast, jump, enemy attacks and thrown shoes.
- **The class of the hit that KILLS decides how the character dies.** Examples: a rocket that doesn't kill and then an AK
  that does = light death; the AK first and a rocket last = heavy death.

## Death animations
- **Heavy weapons:** the red goo explosion from before (the animation, flying chunks, mist, puddle). Only for heavy kills now.
- **Light weapons (redone after your second picture):**
  - The character is hit (white flash), tips over and falls (toward the shot: the sheet falls to the right and is mirrored when
    the hit came from the right), flattens onto a spreading pool of blood and collapses into a **bloody heap built from the
    character's own colors**. It is a corpse that **stays on the ground for 12 s** and then fades (at most 50 corpses at once).
  - **A fine mist of tiny red droplets** sprays out of the wound over a wide area, mostly in the direction of the hit, and
    **hundreds of single red pixels stay scattered on the ground** (densest near the body, with drips; 18 s, at most 80 of them).
  - Higher rarity = bigger and more blood.
- **Player:** enemies hit with light attacks, so the player is shot down and leaves a corpse; game over comes 1 s later as
  before. (A heavy hit would make the player explode; no enemy has a heavy attack yet.)
- Sheets: `assets/characters/death/<name>_death_heavy.png` and `<name>_death_light.png` (generated by
  `tools/make_death_art.py` and `tools/make_death_light_art.py`). `EnemyStats` has `death_sheet_heavy` and `death_sheet_light`, the
  player has the same two exports, so a new character needs both sheets.

## Coin counter (HUD, bottom right)
- The "Coins: n" text is gone. A **new gold coin sprite, exactly as big as the bottle** (16 x 22 art pixels at the same 3x scale), sits
  to the left of the bottle, with **"x" and the amount** next to it in a chunky orange-gradient pixel font with a dark
  outline, like your first picture. The coin looks like the pickup coin (gold, darker inner ring, dark outline, highlight).
- **The coin spins once (12 frames, half a second) every time the amount goes up** (a pickup), and the digits hop a little.
  Picking up coins quickly keeps it spinning. Spending coins in the shop doesn't spin it.
- `ui/coin_counter.gd` is a normal control; the art is `assets/ui/coin_spin.png` and `coin_digits.png`
  (`tools/make_coin_art.py`). The numbers grow to the left, so even 5 digits fit.
